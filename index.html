Standard Operating Procedures
Good Principles
Production & quality
1
Plan the next day's production before the end of each working day.
2
Forecast customer urgency and prioritize production accordingly.
3
Ensure consistent quality checks at every stage and on every machine output.
4
Keep machines, tools, and work areas clean and organized (5S).
5
Carry out preventive maintenance on every machine at least once a month.
6
Communicate dispatch schedules and expected delivery timelines well in advance.
7
Record production, wastage, and delays immediately after every job.
Accounting & finance
1
Record every financial transaction on the same day.
2
Reconcile bank accounts daily or at least once every week.
3
Monitor receivables regularly and follow up on invoices overdue by 30 days.
4
Review daily cash flow and upcoming payment commitments.
5
Complete all statutory compliances well before their due dates.
6
Verify supplier invoices and customer collections before closing each day.
7
Review key financial KPIs and outstanding balances with management every week.
